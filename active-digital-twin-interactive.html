Model assumptions and equation guide

This is an educational animation of a proposed research system, not a trained AI system, operational twin or validated damage detector. It uses synthetic observations and one bending mode of a simply supported 30 m bridge. Parameters and costs are illustrative.

Mechanics, equations 1–4. The slow branch is used: q = (P sin(πx/L) + 0.6 ΔT) / [50(1−d)] in mm, with P in kN and 50 kN/mm baseline modal stiffness. The 0.6 kN/°C thermal coefficient represents an illustrative equivalent gradient load; uniform temperature does not automatically cause vertical bending in a free bridge. Support settlement adds a linear prescribed displacement. The bearing scenario adds an illustrative temperature-dependent modal response. Only the selected condition is applied. Dead-load displacement is removed by the reference state. This animation does not integrate vibration or contact dynamics.

Observations, equations 5–6. The satellite view is 35° from vertical. With downward-positive model displacement, yLOS = −u cos(35°), so positive LOS is toward the satellite. Here u is a reference-epoch displacement change. A single LOS cannot recover three-dimensional displacement. Synthetic field measurements add a deterministic 0.10 mm oscillation to the vertical response. Actual acquisition delays and shared atmospheric errors are not simulated.

Surrogate, equations 7–10. Linear interpolation of 1/(1−d) on 0.1-spaced stiffness-loss knots emulates an approximate response surrogate; it is not a trained neural network. The displayed force residual is 50(1−d)qs − P sin(πx/L) − 0.6ΔT. The original document proposes dimensionless scaled data and physics losses for training and validation.

Updating, equations 11–14. A scalar Gaussian mean update illustrates the ensemble Kalman filter principle: G = 1.4²/(1.4²+0.5²). The forecast holds the normal-condition baseline fixed. The innovation statistic is J = (y−yf)²/(1.4²+0.5²). This does not run the full perturbed-observation ensemble filter. No field alarm threshold is claimed.

Diagnosis, equation 15. Four candidate hypotheses start with equal priors. Their relative probabilities follow Gaussian likelihoods with a common 1.49 mm standard deviation. They are illustrative, conditional on this deliberately restricted candidate set, and not calibrated field probabilities. Ambiguity may remain.

Active sensing, equations 16–17. Expected mutual information I(H;Y) is integrated numerically for three hypothetical Gaussian measurement channels. It is divided by relative costs 1, 1.4 and 2. Bearing, survey and strain channels use illustrative response signatures and noise scales. Taking a measurement adds its synthetic likelihood to the displayed diagnosis. This demonstrates one-step discrete hypothesis learning, rather than the document's full joint condition-parameter information gain. No bridge is controlled.

Verification, equation 18. RMSE, false-alert episodes per monitored bridge-year, detection delay and uncertainty coverage require independent evaluation; they are not asserted by this animation.
