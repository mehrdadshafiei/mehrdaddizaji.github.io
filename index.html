About me

Mehrdad Shafiei Dizaji, Ph.D.

Structural Engineering · AI · NDE · Digital Twins

I am a civil and structural engineering researcher developing data-driven and physics-informed methods for structural health monitoring, nondestructive evaluation, infrastructure inspection, digital twins, and intelligent sensing.

My work connects structural mechanics, computer vision, deep learning, finite element modeling, and sensor data to create practical tools for infrastructure assessment, damage detection, and structural decision-making.

Ph.D. · University of Virginia Postdoctoral Research · FHWA / UVA Structural Health Monitoring & AI

Research Areas

Core expertise

AI for Structural Engineering

Deep learning, engineered neural networks, transfer learning, and intelligent structural identification.

Structural Health Monitoring

Full-field sensing, damage detection, structural identification, computer vision, and image-based diagnostics.

Nondestructive Evaluation

AI-assisted NDE interpretation, visual question answering, GPR data analysis, laser scanning, and automated inspection.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks

Physics-constrained learning for subsurface sensing and prediction, including electromagnetic-wave-informed modeling.

Digital Twins & Mixed Reality

Adaptive digital twins, immersive structural visualization, AR/VR, and cyber-physical infrastructure systems.

Finite Element & Bridge Engineering

Finite element model updating, bridge load rating, structural optimization, testing, and sensor-data integration.
